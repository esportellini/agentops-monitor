# Roadmap

AgentOps Monitor v0.2 includes secure ingest, authoritative pricing, runtime policies, human approvals, in-app alerts, evaluations with an optional OpenAI provider, a reproducible Docker demo, and the operations console.

Future work may include:

- external Slack, email, and webhook notification channels;
- distributed background evaluation execution;
- SSO through SAML or OIDC;
- OpenTelemetry ingestion and export;
- advanced cost and behavior anomaly detection;
- production orchestration, backup, and enterprise deployment guidance;
- configurable data residency and field-level infrastructure encryption;
- richer collaboration and organization hierarchy.

No dates are committed for roadmap items.
